This policy describes what Sticker Showdown processes when you open the game, create a room or play a match. It is written to match how the current version of the software actually behaves. If the software changes, this page is reviewed and updated before the change goes live.

1. Who is responsible

The game is operated by {{OPERATOR}}. The operator decides how the game is run and is the data controller for the information described here. Privacy questions and requests go to {{CONTACT}}.

This is an independent project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to WhatsApp or Meta.

2. What the game processes

Display name

You choose a display name. It is sent to the server, stored in the temporary room state, shown to the other player in that room, and kept in your browser only to fill the name field on your next visit. No account, email address, phone number or password is required or collected.

Temporary match information

While a room exists, the server keeps the room code, player identifiers, connection and ready state, the selected round length, round timestamps and scores in memory. Room and player identifiers are random values generated by the server.

Network information and logs

The server receives the IP address of your connection. The address is used in memory to apply connection and rate limits and to protect the service from abuse. The hosting provider also processes standard request logs such as IP address, time, requested path and user agent. Application logs record event names, counts and error codes. They do not record names, room codes or message payloads.

Browser storage

The game writes a small record to your browser's local storage and keeps a per-tab session entry while you play. The details are listed on the Cookie Preferences page. The game does not set cookies, does not use advertising or analytics trackers, and does not sell personal information.

What the game does not collect

  • No email addresses, phone numbers, postal addresses or passwords.
  • No payment details. The game has no payments, subscriptions or purchases.
  • No contact lists, camera, microphone or precise location access.
  • No advertising identifiers or cross-site tracking.

3. Why the information is used

  • To create rooms and run a live match between two players.
  • To keep the official score and round timing on the server.
  • To reconnect you to a match after a short connection loss.
  • To rate limit requests and reduce abuse and automated traffic.
  • To keep a personal best on your own device when you ask the game to do so.
  • To diagnose faults and keep the service working.

Where data protection law requires a legal basis, the game relies on the performance of the service you request, the legitimate interests of the operator in running a safe and functional game, and your consent where a feature explicitly asks for it. The game does not rely on consent for essential technical processing, because the processing is required to provide the match you asked for.

4. Retention

Room state is temporary. Idle rooms expire after roughly twenty minutes of inactivity and finished rooms are kept for a few minutes so both players can read the result and request a rematch. Expiry values are configurable by the operator. All rooms are discarded when the server process restarts, which happens during deployments and maintenance.

The personal best record stays in your browser until you clear it in the game or clear browser data. Hosting provider logs are kept according to that provider's own retention settings. Application logs are kept only as long as needed for operations and troubleshooting.

5. Sharing and processors

The other player in your room sees your display name, ready state, connection state and score. The game uses the following service providers to operate:

  • Render hosts the web service and may process request logs.
  • GitHub hosts the source code and the sticker assets. If an operator configures a remote sticker manifest, your browser requests images from the configured asset host.

These providers process data on their own infrastructure and may transfer data internationally. No personal information is sold or shared for advertising. Information may be disclosed where required by law or to protect the service and its players.

6. Your choices and rights

You can clear your personal best, and the display name saved for convenience, from the Cookie Preferences page or through your browser settings. Leaving a room removes your player slot from the active room. You can also leave the page at any time.

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information, to object to or restrict processing, or to complain to a supervisory authority. Because the game has no accounts, the operator may be unable to identify a specific player after a room has expired. Requests can still be sent to {{CONTACT}} and will be answered where the operator can reasonably act on them.

7. Children and age

The game is intended for people aged 13 or older, or the minimum age for digital consent in the country where they live, whichever is higher. The game does not knowingly collect personal information from younger children. If you believe a child below that age has played, contact {{CONTACT}} so the room information can be removed.

8. Security

The game keeps the score on the server, validates every message, limits request rates and restricts each connection to its own room. No system is perfectly secure. Do not enter sensitive personal information as a display name, because the other player in your room will see it.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the game changes, for example when features or service providers change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated at the same time. Continued use after an update means the updated policy applies to new sessions.
